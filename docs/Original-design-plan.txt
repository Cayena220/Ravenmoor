1. GAME OVERVIEW
=====================================================================================

Title (working):  Wandbound: Ravenmoor Academy
Pitch:            Enroll at a hidden school of magic, learn real spells in class, master
                  them against monsters in the wilds, and prove yourself against other
                  students in the dueling arena.
Genre:            Action RPG with PvE (monsters, bosses, dungeons) and PvP (duels,
                  ranked arena, open-PvP wilds, house battles).
Platforms:        PC, mobile, console (gamepad). Mobile first for performance.
Target players:   Ages 10-16+, fans of wizard-school fantasy and spell combat.
Session length:   20-40 minutes. A class runs every few minutes, so there is always
                  something to do.
Server size:      Main world 30 players. Dungeon 1-4. Ranked arena 2-6.

CORE LOOP

     Attend class --> Learn a spell --> Use it in fights --> Mastery + loot
          ^                                                       |
          |                                                       v
          +------ New zones / higher rank / House Points <--------+

WHAT MAKES IT STAND OUT
  1. Spoken casting: type a spell's incantation in chat to cast it for +10% power.
  2. Classes that actually teach: you learn spells by tracing wand gestures in class,
     not just by buying them.
  3. Combos: spells react with each other (freeze then shock = Shatter).
  4. House rivalry: every action earns House Points for a weekly House Cup.
  5. Fair ranked PvP: the arena levels everyone to the same stats, so skill wins.

2. SETTING, STORY AND NPCs
=====================================================================================

THE WORLD
  Ravenmoor Academy of Arcane Arts sits on a cliff above Mirrorlake, hidden from the
  ordinary world. Students arrive by sky-ferry at Hollowmere Village, a small wizard
  town at the foot of the castle. Under the castle lie seven ancient seals that hold
  back a darkness called the Hollow.

THE VILLAIN
  Malachar Vane, a former Ravenmoor teacher, was expelled for studying the forbidden
  Shadow Arts. He leads a secret group called the Hollow Circle and wants to break
  all seven seals to take the Hollow's power for himself.

MAIN STORY: 7 CHAPTERS (one per school year)
  Ch 1  The Lantern Wakes      (Year 1)  Arrive, get sorted, first wand, first spells.
                                         The Lantern of Ravenmoor flickers: a seal is
                                         weakening in Whisperwood.
  Ch 2  Whispers in the Wood   (Year 2)  Fight through Whisperwood, find the Hollow
                                         Circle's camp, defeat Mother Vexweave who
                                         guards the first seal. Reward: Tonitra.
  Ch 3  The Drowned Archive    (Year 3)  A lost library under Mirrorlake. The Mirrorlake
                                         Leviathan wakes.
  Ch 4  Light in the Frost     (Year 4)  Climb Frostspire Peaks, learn the Spirit Warden
                                         to drive off the Rimewraiths, defeat Glacior.
  Ch 5  Beneath the Stones     (Year 5)  Enter the Sunken Catacombs and face Lich Warden
                                         Morvane, Vane's lieutenant.
  Ch 6  The Marsh Remembers    (Year 6)  Shadowfen Marsh. The Marsh Hag offers you the
                                         Shadow Arts. Your choice changes the dialogue.
  Ch 7  The Seventh Seal       (Year 7)  Final 4-player raid against Malachar Vane.
                                         Graduation ceremony.

  Launch ships with Chapters 1-2. One new chapter comes with each big update.

KEY NPCs
  Headmistress Elowen Thorne    Head of the school, gives main-story quests.
  Professor Ignatia Rook        Hexcraft (attack spells).
  Professor Bram Holloway       Warding (defense spells).
  Professor Wendeline Quill     Charms (utility spells).
  Healer Mabry Fenn             Restoration (healing spells).
  Professor Corvin Ashgrove     Alteration (control spells).
  Master Silas Murk             Potions (cauldron brewing).
  Tobb Brambleworth             Groundskeeper, beast lore, monster hunting quests.
  Odric Oakhart                 Wandmaker in Hollowmere.
  Duelmaster Kestrel Vey        Runs the Dueling Club and the Crucible.
  The Marsh Hag                 Teaches the Shadow Arts (Chapter 6).

3. HOUSES
=====================================================================================

  +------------+-----------+----------------+---------+------------------------------+
  | House      | Trait     | Colors         | Emblem  | Perk (small on purpose)      |
  +------------+-----------+----------------+---------+------------------------------+
  | Emberclaw  | Courage   | crimson / gold | firecat | +5% offensive spell damage   |
  | Stoneroot  | Loyalty   | amber / black  | bear    | +5% max HP and shield power  |
  | Skyquill   | Wisdom    | blue / silver  | owl     | -5% spell cooldowns          |
  | Nightfang  | Ambition  | green / silver | wyvern  | +5% Gilders and XP from duels|
  +------------+-----------+----------------+---------+------------------------------+

  House perks are turned OFF in ranked Crucible matches.

SORTING: THE MIRROR OF CHOOSING
  - Happens in the Great Hall during the tutorial.
  - The player answers 5 personality questions. Each answer adds points to houses.
    Example: "A door is locked. Do you: (a) break it down [Emberclaw], (b) wait for
    your friends [Stoneroot], (c) study the lock [Skyquill], (d) find another way in
    [Nightfang]?"
  - The mirror shows the result. The player may accept it or pick any house.
  - Keep houses balanced: if one house has 35%+ of active players this week, it shows
    a small "this house is crowded" note (never force a house).
  - House change: once per 30 days from the Headmistress.

HOUSE POINTS AND THE HOUSE CUP
  Earn points for your house:
    Attend a class ...................... +5
    Complete a daily quest .............. +10
    Win a duel (Dueling Club) ........... +5
    Win a ranked match .................. +15
    Defeat a boss ....................... +25
    Win a House War ..................... +500 (whole house)
  - Totals are global across all servers. The Cup resets every Sunday 00:00 UTC.
  - The winning house gets: a banner in the Great Hall for a week, +5% XP buff for
    its members, and a badge for players who contributed 100+ points.
  - Each house has its own common room in the castle (decorated in house colors).

TERRAIN WORKFLOW (do the steps in this order)
  1. Greybox: block out every zone with plain parts first. Walk it at player speed.
     Scale guide: player about 5 studs tall, doors 8-10 studs, corridors 12+ studs
     wide (spell combat needs room), arenas 80-120 studs across.
  2. Base terrain: use the Terrain Editor (Generate, or Import a heightmap +
     colormap made in a free tool like Gaea or World Machine).
  3. Sculpt: Grow, Erode, Smooth, Flatten. Make cliffs between zones so players use
     the intended paths.
  4. Paint: paint materials per the zone table. Use MaterialService and
     MaterialVariants to make unique looks (mossy rock, cracked ice, swamp mud).
  5. Water: Terrain water for Mirrorlake and the marsh. Set Terrain.WaterColor,
     WaterReflectance, WaterTransparency and WaterWaveSize per mood.
  6. Foliage: trees and rocks as MeshParts (not hundreds of parts). Turn on
     Terrain.Decoration for animated grass.
  7. Buildings: build modular kits (wall, window, arch, stair, roof pieces) and reuse
     them. This keeps the castle consistent and the memory low.
  8. Lighting: Lighting.Technology = Future. Use Atmosphere, Sky, Bloom,
     ColorCorrection and SunRays. A client script changes Atmosphere and
     ColorCorrection when the player enters a new zone (tween over 2 seconds).

DAY / NIGHT AND WEATHER
  - One in-game day = 20 real minutes (Lighting.ClockTime moves on the server).
  - Night: Moonhowlers deal +30% damage, Lanternwisps glow and are easier to see,
    20% more monsters spawn in Whisperwood.
  - Rain (random, about 20% of the time in Whisperwood and Shadowfen): everyone
    outside is Wet, which makes lightning spells stronger (see combos, Section 6).
  - Snowstorms in Frostspire: lower visibility, players slowly become Chilled unless
    near a campfire.

PERFORMANCE RULES
  - Workspace.StreamingEnabled = true. Important interactables (Waystones, class
    doors) use ModelStreamingMode = Atomic or Persistent.
  - The castle and big landmarks use Model.LevelOfDetail = StreamingMesh, so they
    show as a low-detail shape from far away.
  - Part budget: under 15,000 parts per zone. Prefer MeshParts and reuse meshes.
  - Few real lights: under 30 PointLights/SpotLights visible at once. Use glowing
    Neon parts and LightEmission on particles to fake light.
  - Zone detection: invisible box parts with a "Zone" attribute, checked with
    workspace:GetPartBoundsInBox a few times a second (or the ZonePlus module).

5. CHARACTERS
=====================================================================================

AVATAR
  - R15 rig (needed for the cast animations).
  - Players keep their own Roblox avatar. The game adds a robe in their house colors
    (layered clothing) and their wand in the right hand.
  - Gear has two layers: stat gear and appearance. Players can wear any look they
    like on top of their real gear.

CHARACTER CREATION / FIRST 10 MINUTES (tutorial funnel)
  1. Arrive by sky-ferry at Hollowmere docks (short cutscene).
  2. Oakhart's Wandery: "the wand chooses you". The player gets a random Common wand
     (random wood + core) and learns Scintara, Aegara and Glimmora.
  3. Tailor: pick robe trim and hat style.
  4. Walk up to the castle. Sorting in the Great Hall (Mirror of Choosing).
  5. First class: Charms with Professor Quill (teaches the gesture minigame).
  6. Training Yard: combat tutorial (basic attack, ward, dodge, aiming) vs. dummies.
  7. First quest: help Tobb clear Thornimps at the edge of Whisperwood.

CORE STATS
  HP        Health. At 0 HP you are knocked out (see "Knock-outs" below).
  Mana      Spent to cast spells. Regenerates over time, faster out of combat.
  Stamina   Spent to dodge-roll (35 per dodge). Regenerates quickly.

ATTRIBUTES (2 points per level, free respec once per day at the Library)
  Power      +2% spell damage per point.
  Focus      +5 max mana and +0.2 mana regen per second per point.
  Vitality   +10 max HP per point.
  Agility    +0.2% crit chance and +0.25% move speed per point (move speed cap +15%).

LEVELS AND YEARS
  Levels 1-50, grouped into school years:
    Year 1: levels 1-7      Year 5: levels 29-35
    Year 2: levels 8-14     Year 6: levels 36-42
    Year 3: levels 15-21    Year 7: levels 43-50
    Year 4: levels 22-28
  At level 50 the player can Graduate (Prestige): reset to level 1, keep all spells,
  gear and cosmetics, get a prestige star badge and +2% XP per prestige (max 5).
  The level cap starts at 20 for launch and rises with each update.

KNOCK-OUTS
  - Safe/PvE zones: at 0 HP you are knocked out. After 5 seconds you wake up at the
    last Waystone you touched. You lose nothing.
  - Dungeon: you are "downed" for 15 seconds. A teammate can revive you with
    Resurga. If not, you respawn at the dungeon entrance.
  - Duels: the loser stops at 1 HP. No death, no loss.
  - Shadowfen: you drop 10% of the Gilders you are carrying (not banked) as a bag
    anyone can pick up, then respawn at the Bounty Camp.

WANDS
  Every wand = a wood + a core + a rarity.

  Woods                     Cores
    Ash          +3% Power    Emberplume (firebird feather)   +5% fire and Burn damage
    Willow       +3% healing  Wyrmscale                       +4% all spell damage
    Rowan        +3% wards    Moonmane                        +5% healing and Cleanse
    Blackthorn   +3% crit     Stormglass                      +5% lightning damage
    Silverbirch  +5% mana regen Frostfang                     +5% slow duration
    Ironoak      +5% max HP   Voidpearl                       +5% Shadow Arts damage,
    Heartwood    +2% to all                                   -10% Corruption gain
    (crafted only)

  Rarity      Bonus multiplier   Where it comes from
    Common        x1.0            Starter wand, shop
    Uncommon      x1.25           Shop, normal monsters
    Rare          x1.5            Elite monsters, quests
    Epic          x2.0            Bosses, crafting
    Legendary     x2.5            Rare boss drops, crafting
    Mythic        x3.0            Seasonal top ranks, Chapter finales (unique glow)

  Wand bonus = (wood bonus + core bonus) x rarity multiplier.
  In ranked matches, wand bonuses are capped at Rare.

GEAR SLOTS
  Wand      Main stat source (see above).
  Robe      Vitality and armor.
  Hat       Focus.
  Amulet    Power.
  Ring      One special effect (for example +10% Burn duration, +5% dodge distance).
  Familiar  A pet that follows you and gives one small passive bonus.

FAMILIARS (no paid random eggs; see Section 11)
  Owl         500 Gilders (Familiar Emporium)   +10% Gilders from quests
  Cat         500 Gilders                       +5% stamina regen
  Toad        300 Gilders                       -5% Burn and Chilled duration on you
  Fox         1,500 Gilders                     +3% move speed
  Raven       Chapter 2 quest reward            +5% loot luck
  Salamander  Rotbark Sentinel rare drop        +10% Burn damage
  Wyrmling    Glacior rare drop (Legendary)     +2% all damage
  Familiar bonuses are off in ranked matches. Familiars are drawn only on clients
  (no server cost).

ANIMATIONS TO MAKE (Animation Editor or Moon Animator)
  - Wand idle, walk and run with wand.
  - Cast: quick flick (projectiles), swish (beams), thrust (heavy spells),
    raise (area spells), two-hand channel (Solarion, Animor).
  - Ward pose (shield up), dodge roll, hit reaction, knockdown/get up.
  - Airborne flail (Aerovanta), sleeping (Somnara), frog form (Mutara).
  - Emotes: bow (duel start), cheer, house salute.

  - The Ward slot (right mouse) only accepts Ward spells. Slots 1-4 accept any spell.
  - Camera: over-the-shoulder with a center crosshair (MouseBehavior = LockCenter).
  - Aim assist on gamepad and mobile: projectiles bend slightly toward the nearest
    enemy inside a 10-degree cone in front of the crosshair.

CASTING RULES
  - Each spell has a mana cost, a cooldown, a cast time (most are 0.15-0.3 seconds)
    and a type: Projectile, Beam, Area, Self, Target, Object or Summon.
  - Projectiles travel (no instant hits) so players can dodge, ward or reflect them.
  - You can move while casting most spells. Heavy spells (Solarion, Animor) root
    you while you cast.
  - Silenced: cannot cast. Rooted: cannot move but can cast.
  - Dodge roll: 10 studs, 0.25 seconds where you cannot be hit (0.2 in PvP).

SPOKEN CASTING (chat incantations)
  - Typing a learned spell's incantation in chat (for example "Flamara") casts it at
    where you are aiming, with +10% power and +0.3 seconds cast time.
  - It uses the normal mana and cooldown, so it cannot be spammed.
  - The server detects it (see Section 12), so it cannot be faked.
  - Players can turn this off in Settings so normal chat does not cast spells.

STATUS EFFECTS
  Burn       Damage over time (fire).
  Chilled    30% slower movement (frost).
  Wet        From rain, water or the Leviathan's splash. Lightning does +30%.
  Silenced   Cannot cast spells.
  Rooted     Cannot move.
  Airborne   Lifted up, cannot move or dodge.
  Asleep     Cannot act. Any damage wakes the target.
  Feared     Runs away from the caster, cannot act.
  Shielded   Damage hits the shield first.

  Crowd control (CC) = Silenced, Rooted, Airborne, Asleep, Feared, frog form.
  Diminishing returns: the 2nd CC within 10 seconds lasts 50%, the 3rd lasts 25%,
  then the target is immune to CC for 5 seconds. Bosses are immune to CC.

COMBOS
  Shatter       Chilled target hit by Tonitra: +50% damage and 0.5 second stun.
  Conduct       Wet target hit by any lightning: +30% damage.
  Combust       Burning target hit by Ruptara: the Burn spreads to everyone in the blast.
  Juggle        Airborne target hit by any Hex: +25% damage.
  Anchored      Rooted target hit by Ruptara: no knockback, but +20% damage.
  Players discover combos by playing. When a combo triggers for the first time, the
  name pops up and it is added to the Spellbook's "Combos" page.

CORRUPTION (Shadow Arts cost)
  - A meter from 0 to 100. It drops by 1 per minute (2 per minute inside the castle).
  - 50+: shop prices +20% and your healing spells are 25% weaker.
  - 100: you are Marked. You get a red aura, a bounty is placed on you, and players
    in Shadowfen can attack you even with the level-gap protection.
  - Cleanse it fully with the Purification Shrine quest in the castle chapel.

=====================================================================================
7. LEARNING SPELLS
=====================================================================================

FOUR WAYS TO LEARN A SPELL
  1. Classes (most spells). A professor teaches it in a timed class at the castle.
  2. Spell Tomes. Found in the Library's Restricted Shelf (quest), as monster drops,
     or bought in shops. Reading a tome teaches the spell if your level is high
     enough.
  3. Story quests. Big spells come from the main story (Tonitra, Spirit Warden).
  4. Achievements. First boss kills and first dungeon clears (Murallis, Resurga).
  See Appendix B for where every spell is learned.

CLASSES
  - A class starts every 5 real minutes, rotating through the 6 subjects (Hexcraft,
    Warding, Charms, Restoration, Alteration, Potions).
  - A bell rings and a countdown shows on the HUD. Players walk (or use the castle
    Waystone) to the classroom.
  - Each class lasts about 3 minutes:
      a) Lecture (30s): the professor shows the spell, the gesture and the incantation.
      b) Practice (60s): the gesture tracing minigame.
      c) Test (60s): cast the spell at moving targets. Hit 5 targets to pass.
  - Rewards: XP, +5 House Points, and the spell (if you passed and meet its level).
  - If you already know the spell taught, you get bonus Mastery progress instead.
  - The class teaches the spell that fits the highest Year among the students
    present, with a lower-level spell offered to anyone who is not high enough.

GESTURE TRACING MINIGAME
  - A dotted wand path appears on screen (circle, zigzag, spiral, star...).
  - The player drags the mouse or finger along the path in one stroke.
  - Score = how close the stroke stays to the path (sample 32 points, measure
    distance to the nearest path point). 70% or better passes.
  - Harder spells have longer, more complex gestures. Shadow Arts gestures are
    shown only in Shadowfen.

SPELL MASTERY (getting better at a spell by using it)
  - Every spell has Mastery levels 1-5. You gain progress each time it hits an enemy.
      Mastery 1: start      Mastery 2: 25 hits    Mastery 3: 75 hits
      Mastery 4: 150 hits   Mastery 5: 300 hits
  - Each Mastery level adds +5% to the spell's power (damage, heal or shield).
  - At Mastery 3 and Mastery 5, pick 1 of 2 upgrades for that spell (can be swapped
    later at the Library for Gilders).

  Upgrade examples
    Scintara  M3: Rapid (cooldown 0.45s)          OR  Piercing (passes through 1 enemy)
              M5: Twin Spark (2 bolts, 60% each)  OR  Charged (hold 1s for 3x damage)
    Flamara   M3: Wildfire (Burn spreads nearby)  OR  Searing (Burn +50% damage)
              M5: Twin Flame (2 projectiles)      OR  Firebird Lash (leaves a fire trail)
    Aegara    M3: Sturdy (+50% absorb)            OR  Riposte (blocked damage fires a
                                                      bolt back)
              M5: Lasting (3s duration)           OR  Shared (also shields 1 nearby ally)
    Vitara    M3: Quick Mend (0.1s cast)          OR  Lingering (+3 HP/s for 3s)
              M5: Twin Mend (heals 2 targets)     OR  Overheal (extra heal becomes shield)
  Design the rest the same way: one choice that makes it stronger, one that changes
  how it plays.

WORLD EVENT: THE MIRRORLAKE LEVIATHAN
  - Rises from Mirrorlake every 2 hours for 10 minutes. A server-wide message warns
    players 2 minutes before.
  - Level 30 World Boss. Everyone who joins is level-synced to 30, so new players can
    help too (their stats are scaled up for the event).
  - Its splash attacks make everyone Wet, so lightning spells do extra damage (the
    event teaches the combo system).
  - Rewards are based on contribution (damage, healing, shielding). Everyone who helps
    gets at least a chest.
  - Castle Grounds stay a safe zone during the event (no PvP).

MONSTER AI
  States: Idle -> Patrol -> Chase -> Attack -> Return
    Idle/Patrol    Wanders near its spawner.
    Chase          A player came within aggro range (15-30 studs, depends on type)
                   or attacked it.
    Attack         In range: uses its attacks with cooldowns.
    Return         Too far from its spawner (leash 80 studs) or target lost. Walks
                   back and heals to full.
  Threat: the monster attacks whoever has the most threat. Damage = 1 threat per
  point, healing an ally = 0.5 threat per point, Aegara/Murallis on an ally = taunt.

SPAWNERS
  - Invisible parts in Workspace/MonsterSpawners with attributes:
      MonsterId = "Gloomspider", MaxAlive = 4, RespawnTime = 30, Radius = 40
  - The server only spawns monsters near players (skip spawners with no player
    within 300 studs). Cap: 25 active monsters per zone.
  - Elites respawn every 5 minutes, zone bosses every 10 minutes (each player can loot
    a boss once per respawn).

LOOT
  - Each monster has a loot table in ServerStorage (never on the client).
  - Loot is personal: each player who helped gets their own drop roll.
  - Drop example (Gloomspider): Gilders 100%, Spider Silk 60%, Uncommon gear 8%,
    Frigora Spell Tome 1%.
  - Rare drops show a colored beam so players notice them.


=====================================================================================
9. PvP (PLAYER VS PLAYER)
=====================================================================================

SAFE AND PvP ZONES
  - Hollowmere Village, Ravenmoor Castle and Castle Grounds: no PvP (except the House
    War area during the event).
  - Whisperwood and Frostspire: no free-for-all PvP, but players can challenge each
    other to a duel anywhere in these zones.
  - Shadowfen Marsh: open PvP.
  - Everyone gets 5 seconds of spawn protection (ends early if they cast a spell).

DUELING CLUB (friendly duels)
  - In the castle Dueling Club hall, or use the "Challenge to Duel" prompt on another
    player in Whisperwood or Frostspire.
  - Both players bow, a 3-2-1 countdown starts, first to drop to 1 HP loses.
  - Options: best of 1 or best of 3, "Shadow rules" on/off (both must be level 35+).
  - Nothing is lost. The winner gets +5 House Points (max 10 duels per hour count).

THE CRUCIBLE (ranked arena)
  - Join the queue from the Crucible portal in the castle.
  - Modes: 1v1 at launch, 2v2 in Update 1, 3v3 in Update 3.
  - Fair rules: everyone is level-synced to 50 with the same stat budget, wand
    bonuses capped at Rare, no house or familiar perks, no potions, no Shadow Arts.
    Players must still have learned the spells they use, and at least 12 spells to
    queue.
  - Matchmaking rating (MMR) starts at 1000 and uses Elo (see Appendix A).
  - Tiers:
      Bronze Wand    0-1099
      Silver Wand    1100-1299
      Gold Wand      1300-1499
      Platinum Wand  1500-1699
      Diamond Wand   1700-1899
      Archmage       1900+
  - Seasons last 8 weeks. End-of-season rewards: robe trims, wand glow effects,
    titles. Top 100 players get a Mythic wand appearance.
  - Leaving a match early counts as a loss.

SHADOWFEN MARSH (open-PvP wilds)
  - Higher rewards: +50% Gilders and XP from monsters, rare wand cores.
  - Death penalty: drop 10% of carried Gilders (bank your Gilders first).
  - Level-gap protection: you cannot attack a player 10 or more levels below you,
    unless they attacked you first or they are Marked (Corruption 100).
  - Bounties: a player who knocks out 5 players without dying gets a bounty. Whoever
    knocks them out gets the bounty in Gilders. The Bounty Camp board shows the top
    bounties on the server.

HOUSE WAR (event)
  - Every 2 hours (one hour after each Leviathan), for 15 minutes.
  - Happens in the Ruins of Old Ravenmoor (Castle Grounds). Only players who walk in
    take part.
  - 3 control points. Standing on a point with no enemies captures it. Each held point
    gives your house 1 point per second.
  - Players are level-synced to 30 during the event.
  - The winning house gets +500 House Points. Every participant gets XP and a chest.

PvP BALANCE RULES
  - Spell damage against players is x0.7. Healing during PvP is x0.8.
  - Crowd control diminishing returns (see Section 6).
  - Target time-to-kill in a fair 1v1: 30-60 seconds. If fights end faster, lower
    damage. If they drag on, lower healing.
  - Track pick rate and win rate for every spell (Section 16). A spell above 55% win
    rate in ranked gets looked at.

10. PROGRESSION AND ECONOMY
=====================================================================================

XP
  XP needed for the next level = floor(100 x Level^1.5)
    Level 1 -> 2:   100 XP
    Level 10 -> 11: 3,162 XP
    Level 20 -> 21: 8,944 XP
  XP sources: monsters, quests, classes, duels, ranked matches, events.
  Target pace for a normal player: level 10 in about 2 hours, level 20 in about 8-10
  hours.

CURRENCIES
  Gilders     Main currency, earned by playing. Used for shops, crafting, respecs.
  Starshards  Premium currency, bought with Robux (also given in small amounts by the
              Spellbook Pass). Used only for cosmetics and convenience.

  Gilder sinks (to keep the economy healthy): wand and gear shop, crafting fees,
  talent/attribute respecs, robe dyes, familiar purchases, Waystone fast travel
  (25 Gilders), Mastery upgrade swaps.

VAULTKEEP BANK
  - In Hollowmere. Deposited Gilders are safe and are never dropped in Shadowfen.
  - Extra item storage tabs (first tab free, more tabs with Gilders or a game pass).

QUESTS
  Main      The 7 chapters (Section 2). Each chapter = 6-10 quests.
  Side      NPC errands. Example: Master Murk needs 10 Spider Silk.
  Daily     3 random tasks, reset every day. Example: defeat 15 Thornimps, win 2
            duels, brew 1 potion.
  Weekly    Bigger tasks. Example: defeat Mother Vexweave 3 times, attend 10 classes,
            take part in a House War.
  Objective types to build: Kill, Collect, Talk to, Reach a place, Cast a spell,
  Win a duel, Brew a potion, Attend a class.

CRAFTING SKILLS (each has levels 1-10, separate from character level)
  Herbology     Gather herbs in the Greenhouses and the wilds (Frost Lily in
                Frostspire, Bog Myrtle in Shadowfen). Higher level = more per pick.
  Potioncraft   Brew potions at a cauldron (see below).
  Wandcrafting  Unlocks at level 20. Combine a wood and a core at Oakhart's workbench.
                Material tier and skill level decide the wand's rarity.

POTIONS (cauldron minigame)
  Health Draught     Thorn Barb + Spider Silk       Heals 40% HP over 4s.
  Mana Tonic         Wisp Essence + Spider Silk     Restores 50 mana.
  Swiftness Elixir   Moonhowler Fang + Wisp Essence +20% move speed for 30s.
  Ironskin Brew      Snowmaw Pelt + Frost Lily      -15% damage taken for 60s.
  Minigame: add ingredients in the right order, then stir with 3 timed button presses
  on a moving bar. Result quality: Common, Fine (+15% effect) or Perfect (+30%).
  Potions have a 30-second shared cooldown and are banned in ranked.

LEADERBOARDS
  All-time: Level, total Prestige, Duels won.
  Weekly/Season: House Points earned, Crucible rating, Leviathan damage.

SERVICE LOADER
  -- ServerScriptService/Main.server.lua
  local services = {}
  for _, module in script.Parent.Services:GetChildren() do
      if module:IsA("ModuleScript") then
          services[module.Name] = require(module)
      end
  end
  for _, service in services do
      if service.Init then service:Init(services) end
  end
  for _, service in services do
      if service.Start then task.spawn(service.Start, service) end
  end
  (Main.client.lua does the same for Controllers.)

CASTING FLOW (server-authoritative)
  1. Client: player presses a key. CastController plays the cast animation and a
     local projectile right away (so it feels instant), then fires CastSpell.
  2. Server: SpellService checks everything (code below). If it fails, it sends
     CastRejected and the client removes its local effect.
  3. Server: spends mana, starts the cooldown, then simulates the projectile itself:
     every Heartbeat it moves the projectile forward (speed x dt) and checks for hits
     with workspace:Spherecast. No projectile Part is created on the server.
  4. Server: fires SpellFx to all other clients so they draw the same projectile.
  5. Server: on a hit, CombatService.ApplyDamage runs the formula, the target's shield
     absorbs first, statuses are applied, and DamageNumber is sent.
  6. If hits feel unfair for players with high ping, later add "rewind": store each
     target's positions for the last 0.5s and check the hit against where the target
     was when the caster fired.

CAST VALIDATION (example)
  -- ServerScriptService/Services/SpellService.lua (excerpt)
  local MAX_AIM_SLACK = 15 -- extra studs allowed beyond the spell's range

  local function isBadVector(v)
      return v.X ~= v.X or v.Y ~= v.Y or v.Z ~= v.Z -- NaN check
  end

  local function validateCast(player, spellId, aimPos)
      if typeof(spellId) ~= "string" or typeof(aimPos) ~= "Vector3" then
          return false, "BadInput"
      end
      if isBadVector(aimPos) then return false, "BadInput" end

      local spell = SpellData[spellId]
      if not spell then return false, "UnknownSpell" end

      local data = PlayerDataService.GetData(player)
      if not data or not data.Spells[spellId] then return false, "NotLearned" end

      local state = CombatService.GetState(player)
      if not state or state.KnockedOut or state.Statuses.Silenced then
          return false, "CannotCast"
      end
      if state.Mana < spell.ManaCost then return false, "NoMana" end

      local now = os.clock()
      if now < (state.Cooldowns[spellId] or 0) then return false, "OnCooldown" end

      local rules = ZoneService.GetRules(player)
      if spell.School == "Shadow" and not rules.AllowShadowArts then
          return false, "NotAllowedHere"
      end

      local root = player.Character and player.Character:FindFirstChild("HumanoidRootPart")
      if not root then return false, "NoCharacter" end
      if (aimPos - root.Position).Magnitude > spell.Range + MAX_AIM_SLACK then
          return false, "OutOfRange"
      end

      state.Mana -= spell.ManaCost
      state.Cooldowns[spellId] = now + spell.Cooldown
      return true
  end

RANKED MATCHMAKING
  - MemoryStoreService sorted map "Ranked1v1": key = userId, sort value = MMR.
  - Every few seconds a server reads the map and looks for 2 players within +/-100
    MMR. The range widens by 50 every 10 seconds of waiting.
  - It claims both players with UpdateAsync (so two servers cannot grab the same
    player), calls TeleportService:ReserveServer for the Crucible place, and
    teleports both with TeleportAsync (TeleportOptions.ReservedServerAccessCode).
    MessagingService tells the other player's server to teleport them.
  - The Crucible server reports the result and updates both players' MMR.

GLOBAL DATA
  - House Points: each server adds its points to a DataStore key per house with
    UpdateAsync once per minute (not on every point).
  - Weekly/season leaderboards: MemoryStore sorted maps.
  - All-time leaderboards: OrderedDataStore.

PERFORMANCE
  - Object pooling: keep a pool of projectile visuals and effect parts on each client.
    Reuse them instead of creating and destroying.
  - Use ParticleEmitter:Emit(n) bursts instead of always-on emitters.
  - Monsters: the server runs AI 5-10 times per second (not every frame). Animations
    play on clients.
  - Familiars and damage numbers exist only on clients.
  - Send VFX through UnreliableRemoteEvents (they are small and fine to drop).

ANTI-EXPLOIT CHECKLIST
  [ ] Never trust numbers from the client: no damage, HP, mana or cooldowns from it.
  [ ] Check the type of every remote argument (typeof), plus NaN and string length.
  [ ] Rate-limit every remote per player (for example CastSpell max 10 per second).
  [ ] Cooldowns and mana live on the server.
  [ ] Check movement: if a player moves farther than possible between checks,
      pull them back. Blink (Translo) is validated on the server.
  [ ] Loot tables, server configs and admin tools live in ServerStorage /
      ServerScriptService, never in ReplicatedStorage.
  [ ] ProcessReceipt is safe to run twice (PurchaseHistory).
  [ ] Free models: before using one, search it for hidden scripts, require(...) with
      a number, getfenv and loadstring. These are backdoors.
  [ ] Log suspicious events (impossible speed, too many remote calls) with
      TelemetryService and review them.

APPENDIX A. FORMULA SHEET
=====================================================================================

PLAYER
  MaxHP        = 100 + 10 x Level + 10 x Vitality
  MaxMana      = 100 + 2 x Level + 5 x Focus
  ManaRegen/s  = 4 + 0.2 x Focus        (x2 after 5 seconds out of combat)
  Stamina      = 100, dodge costs 35, regen 20/s after 1 second
  WalkSpeed    = 16 x (1 + 0.0025 x Agility), max +15%
  CritChance   = 5% + 0.2% x Agility (max 25%), crit damage x1.5

DAMAGE / HEALING / SHIELDS
  LevelScale   = 1 + 0.08 x (Level - 1)
  Final = Base
        x LevelScale
        x (1 + 0.02 x Power)
        x (1 + 0.05 x (Mastery - 1))
        x (1 + WandBonus + HouseBonus + TalentBonus)
        x ElementOrComboBonus
        x Crit (1.5 if crit)
        x PvPMult (0.7 vs players, 1.0 vs monsters; healing 0.8 in PvP)
        x (1 - TargetResist)
  Example: level 10, Power 10, Mastery 2, Flamara (18 base) vs a monster:
    18 x 1.72 x 1.2 x 1.05 = about 39 damage before the Burn.

COOLDOWNS
  Cooldown = Base x (1 - HouseCDR - TalentCDR), never below 50% of Base

XP AND LEVELS
  XP to next level = floor(100 x Level^1.5)

MONSTERS
  HP      = (50 + 25 x Level) x RankMult
            RankMult: Normal 1, Elite 3, Boss 15, Dungeon Boss 20, World Boss 50
  Damage  = (5 + 2 x Level) x (Normal 1, Elite 1.5, Boss 2.5)
  XP      = (10 + 5 x Level) x (Normal 1, Elite 4, Boss 25)
  Gilders = 2 + Level x (Normal 1, Elite 5, Boss 50)
  Dungeon scaling by party size: 40% / 60% / 80% / 100% for 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 players

RANKED (Elo)
  Expected = 1 / (1 + 10 ^ ((OpponentMMR - YourMMR) / 400))
  NewMMR   = MMR + K x (Result - Expected)     Result: 1 win, 0 loss
  K = 48 for your first 10 games, then 32

CROWD CONTROL
  Diminishing returns within 10 seconds: 100% -> 50% -> 25% -> immune for 5 seconds

=====================================================================================
APPENDIX D. GLOSSARY
=====================================================================================

  Airborne          Status: lifted into the air, cannot move or dodge.
  Corruption        0-100 meter that rises when you use Shadow Arts.
  Crowd control/CC  Effects that stop a player acting (Silenced, Rooted, Asleep...).
  Elo / MMR         Rating number used to match ranked players of similar skill.
  Gilders           The main currency, earned in play.
  House Points      Points players earn for their house. The top house wins the Cup.
  Level Sync        Setting everyone to the same level and stats for fair fights.
  Marked            Corruption at 100. Has a bounty and can be attacked by anyone in
                    Shadowfen.
  Mastery           Levels 1-5 of skill with one spell, earned by hitting with it.
  ProfileStore      Luau module for saving player data with session locking.
  Reserved server   A private server made by script, used for dungeons and ranked.
  Rojo              Tool that syncs code files from VS Code into Roblox Studio.
  Spoken casting    Casting a spell by typing its incantation in chat.
  Starshards        Premium currency bought with Robux, cosmetics only.
  StreamingEnabled  Roblox setting that only loads the parts of the map near you.
  Threat            How much a monster wants to attack you.
  Waystone          Fast-travel stone. Also your respawn point after a knock-out.
